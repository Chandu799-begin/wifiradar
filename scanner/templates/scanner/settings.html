Browser & OS limitations
  • Browsers cannot scan Wi-Fi themselves — all scanning happens on the machine running Django.
  • A hosted website cannot control a visitor's adapter; the local agent is required for connect/disconnect.
  • Signal strength does not encode physical distance or direction — radar placement is illustrative.
  • Windows may require the WLAN AutoConfig service; Linux requires NetworkManager (nmcli).
